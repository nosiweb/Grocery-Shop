## 🌿 About

**Laltu Grocery Shop** is a local grocery business serving customers around **Kanakpur, Bhimpur, West Bengal**.

This project gives the business a modern online presence while keeping the experience simple for local customers.

The website focuses on:

- 🛍️ Everyday grocery products
- 📱 Mobile-friendly browsing
- 📍 Easy location discovery
- ⭐ Google Maps & reviews
- 💬 Customer enquiries
- 🚚 Delivery information
- 📋 Business terms & policies

> **Note:** This is an informational business website. It does **not** provide online checkout or online product sales.
