Delivery & Online Order Policy

Please read the following information before placing an online order or requesting delivery. These guidelines explain our delivery, online ordering and product pickup services.

Delivery Policy

We provide local delivery services for eligible orders, subject to shop operating hours, delivery distance and delivery-person availability.

1. Delivery During Shop Hours

Delivery service is not available at all times. Delivery requests are accepted only during the shop's operating hours.

Requests received outside the operating hours may be considered when the shop reopens.

2. Delivery Area

Delivery is generally available only for addresses located within approximately 500 metres of the shop.

Delivery availability may also depend on the actual location, road accessibility and availability of a delivery person.

3. Delivery Time

We make reasonable efforts to complete deliveries as soon as possible. However, delivery time is not fixed or guaranteed.

The actual delivery time may depend on the availability and capability of the delivery person, order volume, traffic, weather, distance and other local circumstances.

Important: Any estimated or communicated delivery time should not be treated as a guaranteed arrival time.

4. Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery (COD) may be available for selected orders or locations.

COD is a conditional service and is not available for every order or at all times. Availability will depend on the circumstances of the particular order.

Online Order & Pickup Policy

The online ordering facility is provided to help customers select products conveniently before visiting the shop. Orders are subject to shop hours, product availability and confirmation by the shop.

1. Online Orders During Shop Hours

Online orders are accepted during the shop's operating hours.

Orders submitted outside operating hours may be processed when the shop opens again.

2. Shop Pickup

Products ordered through the website are provided primarily on a shop pickup basis.

Customers are required to visit the shop and collect their ordered products.

3. No Automatic Doorstep Delivery

Placing an online order does not automatically create a doorstep delivery request.

Customers must collect their products from the shop unless a separate delivery arrangement has been confirmed by the shop.

4. Payment Verification

If an order has been paid through an online payment method, the customer may be requested to provide reasonable proof of payment when collecting the order.

This may include a payment confirmation, transaction reference or other relevant payment information.

5. Product Collection

Customers should collect their orders during the shop's operating hours.

The order will be made available for collection after it has been confirmed and prepared by the shop.

6. Product Availability

An online order request does not guarantee that every requested product will be available.

Product availability may change before an order is prepared. If a requested product is unavailable, the customer may be informed and an alternative arrangement may be discussed.

General Conditions

  • Online orders are accepted during the shop's operating hours.
  • Delivery service is subject to shop operating hours.
  • Local delivery is generally limited to approximately 500 metres from the shop.
  • Delivery time is not guaranteed.
  • Delivery timing depends on the circumstances and availability of the delivery person.
  • Cash on Delivery may be available only in selected circumstances.
  • Website orders are primarily intended for pickup from the shop.
  • Online ordering does not automatically include doorstep delivery.
  • Customers may be asked to provide payment proof when collecting an order paid online.
  • Product availability may change and is subject to confirmation by the shop.
